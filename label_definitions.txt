troubleshooting_request: asks for help diagnosing or fixing a current hardware fault or malfunction
information_request: asks for hardware instructions, specifications, compatibility, recommendations, or reference information without centering on a current fault
technical_response_report: explains, answers, evaluates, argues about, or reports hardware experience without making a primary request for help
